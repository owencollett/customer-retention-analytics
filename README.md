# Customer Retention Analytics

## Goal
Identify customer characteristics associated with churn and translate findings into business retention recommendations.

## Stack
Python, pandas, matplotlib.

## Dataset
Synthetic dataset of 6,000 customers including tenure, monthly charge, support usage, contract type, autopay, plan type, and churn.

## Resume bullets — use only after you run and understand the project
- Analyzed 6,000 customer records in Python/pandas to identify behavioral and account characteristics associated with churn.
- Built reusable data-cleaning, aggregation, and visualization workflows to compare retention patterns across customer segments.
- Translated analytical findings into retention recommendations focused on contract structure, support experience, and customer tenure.

## Interview topics
Know what churn means, why correlation is not causation, which segments differ most, and what additional data you would request in a real business setting.
